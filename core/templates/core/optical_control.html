    📐 Plan Station Layout

    A starting suggestion for where to put each station - walk it off with paces, no tape measure needed.

    1. Pick how many stations you have.
    2. Enter about how high you expect the rocket to fly - the suggested distances scale to this.
    3. Figure out your pace length: walk 10 normal steps in a straight line, measure the distance in meters, divide by 10, and type that in.
    4. For each station: stand at the pad, face the compass bearing shown (use your phone's compass), and walk the number of paces shown - that's the spot.
    5. On that station's phone, open the Position step and type in the East and North meters shown for that station - not the paces, the app itself needs meters.
    6. If a spot's blocked (a tree, fence, etc.), it's fine to move a few meters - just note how far off you ended up and adjust the East/North numbers to match.
    Unsure? A typical water rocket often reaches roughly 30-60m - adjust if yours flies higher or lower.
